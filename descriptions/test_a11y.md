# Batch A: accessibility guidelines only

## Still Life (Pieter Claesz, c. 1625)
A table of food and drink stands against a black background. In the centre, a tall round pie on a grey metal plate is cut open, showing a dark filling and a slice of lemon. In front, a half-peeled lemon and thin lemon slices lie on a plate. On the left are a large green glass, olives and a plate of bread and sweets. On the right, a glass lies tipped against a grey metal jug beside nuts, a knife and a bread roll on a red cloth.

## Adoration of the Magi (Jan van Scorel, c. 1519)
A seated woman in a white veil and blue robe holds a naked baby on her lap in a ruined courtyard. An old bald man in a gold patterned cloak kneels to kiss the baby's hand, his crown on the ground beside him. On the left, a dark-skinned man in pink holds a round gold vessel. On the right, a man in a red cloak carries a tall gold cup, followed by a young attendant. Behind them, riders cross green hills towards a town under a single star.

## Love of Winter (George Bellows, 1914)
Crowds skate on a frozen pond under dark blue hills patched with snow. In the foreground, a person in a mustard-yellow coat and dark hat walks across the snow, holding hands with a child in a red coat. On the left, a person in a long red coat stands with a fair-haired child. On the right, a group in dark coats bends over a small child dressed in white. Behind them, dozens of skaters in black, green and red lean forward as they glide across the ice.

## The Bedroom (Vincent van Gogh, 1889)
A small bedroom with pale blue walls and a pink and green floor is seen from the doorway. A large wooden bed in bright orange-brown fills the right side, made up with yellow pillows and a red cover. On the left stand two yellow wooden chairs with straw seats and a small table holding a jug, a basin and bottles. A green window is half open at the back. Pictures and portraits hang on the walls, and clothes hang from a row of pegs above the bed.

## The Family Concert (Jan Steen, 1666)
A group of people make music around a table in a large, dim room. On the left, a smiling person in a white shirt and black hat sits on a window seat playing a lute. Around the table covered in a patterned rug, others sing, read music and play a pipe. A small child in a red cap plays a large stringed instrument taller than themself. In the foreground, a brown and white dog sniffs the floor while a grey cat drinks from a metal dish. A servant enters from a doorway on the right.
