# Batch B: accessibility guidelines plus object focus

## Still Life (Pieter Claesz, c. 1625)
A table of food and drink stands against a black background. In the centre, a tall round pie sits on a grey metal plate, cut open to show a dark filling and a lemon slice. In front, lemon slices and a half-peeled lemon lie on a plate. On the left are a large glass of water, a dish of olives and two flat brown cookies. On the right, a wine glass lies tipped against a grey metal jug, beside scattered nuts, a knife and a bread roll.

## Adoration of the Magi (Jan van Scorel, c. 1519)
Mary, in a white veil and blue robe, holds the naked Christ Child on her lap in a ruined courtyard beside a donkey. An old Magus in a gold patterned cloak kneels to kiss the child's hand, his crown on the ground. On the left, a Magus in pink holds a glass orb topped with a small cross. On the right, a Magus in a red cloak carries a tall golden vessel, followed by a page boy. Behind them, soldiers on horseback ride towards a castle on a hill, beneath the Star of Bethlehem.

## Love of Winter (George Bellows, 1914)
Crowds skate on a frozen pond below dark blue, snow-covered hills, with a small house and conifers on the slopes. In the foreground, a person in a mustard-yellow coat walks across the snow, holding hands with a child in a red coat. On the left, a person in a long red coat stands with a fair-haired child. On the right, people in dark coats bend over a small child in a white coat. Behind them, dozens of skaters in black, green and red glide across the ice.

## The Bedroom (Vincent van Gogh, 1889)
A small bedroom with pale blue walls and a pink and green floor is seen from the door. A large orange-brown bed fills the right side, with two yellow pillows and a red blanket. On the left are two yellow chairs with straw seats and a small table holding a jug, a bowl and bottles. A towel hangs by the door, a mirror and a green window are on the back wall, and clothes hang on pegs. Framed pictures hang above the bed, including two portraits of faces.

## The Family Concert (Jan Steen, 1666)
A group of people make music around a table in a large, dim room. On the left, a smiling person in a white shirt and black hat sits on a window seat playing a lute, a wine glass on the sill and a windmill visible through the window. Around a table with a fringed tablecloth and an open music book, others sing and play a recorder. A small child in a red cap plays a viola da gamba taller than themself. A violin and a framed painting hang on the wall. In the foreground, a brown and white dog sniffs at a broken jug while a cat drinks from a metal plate. A servant carrying a glass on a tray enters from a doorway.
